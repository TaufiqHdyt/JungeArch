Imah Bata Villa is a second home in Sindanglaya, Cimenyan, Bandung. Designed without air conditioning, the residence uses natural airflow and the cooler village climate to create a restorative escape from the city.

Exposed brick gives the villa its defining texture inside and out. Traditional materials are composed within a contemporary form, complemented by timber floors, warm ceilings, generous daylight, and open living spaces.

The garden, terrace, pond, and ground-floor lounge extend everyday life into the landscape—creating a place for quiet conversation, fishing, and unhurried weekends.
